Brain:

A special Vinyl concept, "brains" are chunks of code that manage audio for you. Each type of brain
does something different, please refer to individual documentation for details. Some brains will
run an update function every frame, some brains will only do something when you execute a method.
Other brains act as playable audio. There's a lot of variety. Brains can be used together or
separately as the situation demands.



Voice:

There are two kinds of voice that Vinyl uses. The simplest is a number that GameMaker uses to
identify each individual playing sound. As an analogy, a voice is an "instance" of a sound asset
"object". Voices are what is returned by `audio_play_sound()`.

The second type of voice that is unique to Vinyl is a brain voice. Some brains can be used directly
with Vinyl functions that otherwise require native GameMaker voices.



Playable:

Data that can be played by Vinyl's audio functions. The most type of "playable" is a sound asset
added to your game via the IDE. Other playables include certain brains, such as `VinylBrainHLT()`.
Most functions can also handle an array of playables.



Mix Group:

A collection of sound assets that can be manipulated together. Mix groups are usually used for
controlling gain of specific types of sound. For example, all ambient sounds might be in an
"ambient" mix group. It's common to give players the option to adjust the gain of individual types
of audio separately and mix groups gives you that option.
